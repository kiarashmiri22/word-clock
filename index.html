Our Philosophy

A typographic approach to temporal awareness.

Our handmade word clocks are engineered to alter how we interact with the passing of hours. Strikingly physical yet inherently minimalist, the timepiece removes the anxiety of ticks, and instead frames the day in standard English literary syntax.

Behind the precision-crafted solid oak casing and laser-etched facade lies an array of high-grade, energy-efficient matrices designed to wash the letters in a soft, atmospheric glow.

“Words instead of numbers.”

Good to know

Frequently asked

Everything about ordering, customisation, and living with a Halden word clock. Still unsure? We are one message away.

How long does each clock take to make?

Every piece is handmade to order. Production typically takes 4–6 weeks, followed by insured worldwide shipping. You'll receive updates at each stage of the build.

Can I customise the wood, size, or language?

Yes. Beyond the three standard editions we offer bespoke sizes, alternative timbers, and matrices in several languages. Mention your wishes in the inquiry form and we'll prepare a quote.

What is the warranty and after-care?

Each Halden clock is covered by a 5-year warranty on electronics and craftsmanship. The matrix components are rated for decades of continuous use, and we offer lifetime servicing.

Do you ship worldwide, and what about returns?

We ship worldwide with fully insured, tracked carriers. As pieces are made to order, we accept returns within 14 days of delivery for standard editions in original condition.
